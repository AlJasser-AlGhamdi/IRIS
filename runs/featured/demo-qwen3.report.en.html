Cybersecurity policy

We prepared this draft because your answer in the assessment was: "No".

Purpose

This policy protects the information, systems and services of Wahat Al-Ramal Trading from unauthorised access, disruption and leaks, and sets out who is responsible.

Scope

All staff (about 40 employees) and the devices, accounts and cloud services the business uses, including the domains: wahat-alramal.test, www.wahat-alramal.test.

Responsibility

The general manager appoints [............] as the person responsible for cybersecurity. They follow up on this policy and send management a summary every three months.

Rules

  • Every employee has their own account, and two-factor authentication is mandatory for email, remote access and admin accounts.
  • Devices and systems are updated regularly, and malware protection is active on every device.
  • No internal service (databases, printers, remote access) is opened to the internet except over VPN.
  • Regular backups are taken and restores are tested.
  • Every employee gets awareness training on joining and regular phishing reminders.
  • Any employee reports any incident or suspicion immediately, following the incident response plan.

Review

The policy is reviewed every year or after any major change. Next review: 2027-10-30.
