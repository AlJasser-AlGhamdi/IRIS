      How to read the replay

      Iris assesses a fictional small company in a controlled test environment. The replay preserves the recorded AI choices, scan evidence, approvals, fixes, and verification results.

      • Conversation records what Iris asks and what the owner answers.
      • Control map gives every NCA control its own tile and evidence state.
      • Evidence keeps external observations separate from owner documentation.
      • AI decisions shows the allowed menu and the code verdict for each choice.
